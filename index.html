Fight Club.

Tiny wings. Big grudges.

  1. Hold the light. On your turn each of your moths moves, then gusts, punches, zaps or dances.
  2. Survive the reply. A lamp scores for you if you still have more moths in its light after your rival's turn.
  3. Mind the hand. Each round a giant hand hangs over the busiest lamp, then swats it. Loud noises draw it.

First to ten wins.
