Moth
Fight Club.

Tiny wings. Big grudges.

  1. Hold the light. On your turn each of your moths moves, then gusts, punches, zaps or dances. Knock a rival off the desk and it misses its next round.
  2. Survive the reply. A lamp scores for you if you still have more moths in its light after your rival's turn.
  3. Keep moving. The pulsing lamp pays double this round. A lamp that pays you twice running burns out for a round.
  4. Mind the hand. Each round a giant hand hangs over the busiest desk lamp, then swats it. Flutter up to the high lamps and it cannot reach you, but it is a long way down.

First to ten wins.
