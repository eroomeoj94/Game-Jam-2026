Tiny wings. Big grudges.

Two teams of moths, seven lamps. On your turn each of your moths moves and takes one action. A lamp scores for you if you still have more moths in its light after your rival's turn. Flip a lamp into a bug zapper and anyone in its light costs their team a point. Each round a giant hand hangs over the busiest lamp, then swats it. First to ten wins.

One player: you are Mustard and the computer plays Teal.
